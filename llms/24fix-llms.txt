# 24 FIX

> 24 FIX is the flagship consumer home-maintenance brand of 24 Solution Group (Thailand) Co., Ltd.,
> founded in Bangkok in 2018 and now operating under the 24X umbrella brand. It delivers end-to-end
> property maintenance — air-conditioner cleaning and repair, appliance service, electrical, plumbing,
> painting, cleaning, renovation and construction — through an in-house managed network of more than
> 500 fixer teams and material vendor stores, rather than acting as a matchmaking marketplace.

## Status for agents and integrators

24 FIX publishes **no public API**. This profile records a measured absence, not an unfinished search.
As of 2026-09-05, across every host the company operates (24x.co.th, www.24x.co.th, fix.24x.co.th,
business.24x.co.th, housesolution.24x.co.th, projects.24x.co.th, careers.24x.co.th):

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, WSDL or Protobuf contract at any probed location.
- No `/apis.json`, `/apis.yml` or `/.well-known/apis.json`.
- No `/.well-known/` discovery document of any kind — every named path returns a genuine 404, and the
  negative control 404s too.
- No `/llms.txt`, no MCP server, no A2A agent card.
- No developer portal, API reference, SDK, CLI or webhook catalog; the words "API", "SDK", "developer"
  and "webhook" do not appear in the served HTML of any 24X property.
- The only `/api/` path in the estate is the internal Next.js route tree of the fix.24x.co.th booking
  app, which the site's own robots.txt disallows. It is an application internal, not a product.
- The legacy brand domain 24fix.co is a parked GoDaddy domain and serves nothing but a lander redirect.

Integration with 24 FIX is a commercial conversation, not a self-serve one:
business enquiries go to business@24fix.co.
